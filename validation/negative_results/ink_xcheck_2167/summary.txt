Findings
- Every different-winding pair outside the gap sits at 0.0-0.16. w034-w035 is the exception (0.2-0.47 everywhere, highest at high z):
  it behaves as a same/overlapping-layer pair, so it was excluded from the null controls (a post-hoc choice; flagged).
- In the gap, w034-w036 (.22), w036-w037 (.20) and w035-w036 (.18) rise above their own outside-gap values (.09-.14) and above the
  shuffled-z null p95. w034-w037, w035-w037 do not (.10, .05). w033-w036 also rises (.17 vs .04) although w033 is the innermost
  winding and w036 is a different sheet outside the gap, so the rise is not specific to "same sheet".
- By the preregistered rule only w033-w036, w034-w035, w034-w036, w035-w036, w036-w037 pass (all band), but the margin over the
  strongest different-winding control (0.16) is 0.01-0.06, and w033-w036 passing shows the rule is not discriminating here.
- Ceiling: even/odd grid-row split gives r=0.98 but those are adjacent interpolated cells, so it is NOT a same-papyrus ceiling.
  The only real same-sheet reference is w034-w035 (0.33 overall, 0.47 high z), so true same-sheet ink agreement tops out near 0.2-0.5.
- Coverage (frac bins ink>0.5 in the gap): w033 .19, w034 .10, w035 .08, w036 .16, w037 .13; no mesh is ink-empty in the gap.
  w036/w037 have about 3x more ink in the gap than in ctlA/ctlB (.16/.13 vs .05), w034 unchanged.

VERDICT: inconclusive-to-weak. Gap pairs involving w036 (with w034, w035, w037) correlate modestly above controls; w037 does not
correlate with w034 or w035. No pair reaches the w034-w035 same-sheet level (0.33-0.47) in the gap. The ink evidence neither confirms
that w034/w036/w037 are one sheet nor separates them cleanly. Controls separate only weakly.
Caveats: all meshes share one ink model; text-line z-periodicity can correlate different windings (z-roll null addresses only partly);
ink resolution here is 100 um bins from 20x20 block means of the full-res tif (ds8 not used); the gap pairs include meshes that
may sit partly on air where ink prob is near-constant; w035 covers only ~half the angles, so its gap n is small (~22k bins).
