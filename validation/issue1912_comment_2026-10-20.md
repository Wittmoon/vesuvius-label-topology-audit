Following up on my teammate Chris Thomas's (@Baz248) earlier comment ([here](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/issues/1912#issuecomment-6116916788)), we packaged our fine-to-coarse pixel map and checker into a small public repository so anyone moving ink labels, predictions, or tile coordinates between the fine and coarse surface volumes of the same segment can map pixels directly instead of scaling by the voxel ratio. We also completed the catalog-wide mesh check across all 8 scrolls (with a 52-pair held-out test and 154-pair application split) and ran a pre-registered CT check on 7 fresh segments to measure the remaining scan-to-scan transform residual after the pixel map is applied.

We used the formula on every pair we could check (203 of 206) and it passed with zero failures: 52 pairs held out as a test, 151 as an application of the same rule.

Prepared by AI:
- Repo: https://github.com/slchicoineau-creator/tifxyz-canvas-map. `meshpair.canvasmap.fine_to_coarse_px(y, x, fine_mesh_dir, coarse_mesh_dir)` gives the map from the two mesh folders; `results/pair_maps.tsv` lists it for all 207 published pairs (catalog of 2026-10-11).
- Catalog-wide mesh check: on vertices away from the source mesh's grid creases, the fine and coarse meshes follow the formula to float32 precision. 52 held-out pairs: 52/52 pass. The same rule applied to the other 154 pairs (an application, not a test): 151 pass, 3 too small to evaluate (PHerc0500P2), 0 fail.
- Limits: the held-out pairs cover fine/source ratio 4 only (none at ratio 1.95, most of PHerc0500P2); the check is blind to defects on fewer than ~5 % of the clean vertices (9 off-surface vertices found, in 5 pairs).
- CT check: after the formula, the two surface volumes still differ by 0.48-1.46 coarse px (median per segment, 7 pre-registered segments) against 6.3-14.4 px with index pairing. That remainder is the transform's own residual; on PHerc0139 a single affine is borderline (leave-one-out p95 2.05-2.24 px vs a 2.0 px bar).
- The constant part of the formula (about −7.8 coarse px) fits TAUIL's "median −8, −7 native px" above; we did not re-measure his numbers.
